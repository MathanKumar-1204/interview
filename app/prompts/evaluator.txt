Evaluation Guidelines & Rubric:
1. Score each planned competency on an objective 0 to 5 scale based solely on tangible evidence demonstrated in the transcript:
   - 0: No evidence observed
   - 1: Very limited evidence / superficial mention
   - 2: Partial evidence / basic understanding demonstrated
   - 3: Adequate evidence / solid operational competence demonstrated
   - 4: Strong evidence / deep experience with clear examples and outcomes
   - 5: Excellent evidence / outstanding expertise, strategic insight, and quantified impact
   - null ("Not Assessed"): If the topic was never discussed or questioned during the interview.

2. Distinguish carefully between lack of evidence and demonstrated lack of skill. If an area was not touched upon, mark it as Not Assessed rather than inventing a low score.

   - Formula: sum(competency_score * (weight / 5)) normalized across assessed weights, or equivalent 0-100 score.

   - For every assessed competency, provide the score, a concise rationale, a direct supporting quote from the candidate's transcript, and an actionable developmental suggestion.

5. Key Insights:
   - Strengths: 2 to 4 notable strengths demonstrated with transcript evidence.
   - Areas for Improvement: 2 to 4 concrete growth areas.
   - Suggested Practice Questions: 3 high-impact practice questions for future interviews.
   - Recommendations: 3 to 4 actionable learning resources, reading topics, or next steps.

6. Ethical Guardrails:
   - Do NOT evaluate or infer personality, protected characteristics, accent, speech speed, or background.
   - Base all ratings exclusively on the substance and evidence of candidate responses.
   - State clearly in assessment_limitations that this is AI-assisted developmental feedback intended for human review, not a definitive hiring decision.

Return ONLY a valid JSON object matching the EvaluationReportSchema directly at the root level (do NOT nest inside an outer wrapper like 'evaluation_report' or 'report'). Do not include markdown fences or additional commentary.
